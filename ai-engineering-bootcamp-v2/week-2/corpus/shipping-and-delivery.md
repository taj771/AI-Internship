# Northwind Traders — Shipping and Delivery Terms

## Delivery options

| Service | Transit time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 working days | 12.50 euros |
| Express | Next working day | 45.00 euros |
| Refrigerated | 2–3 working days | 68.00 euros |
| Pallet freight | 5–7 working days | quoted per shipment |

Orders over 500 euros qualify for free standard shipping. The threshold is
calculated on the order value before tax and after any discounts. Express and
refrigerated services are charged in full regardless of order value.

## Cut-off times

Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a working day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after 14:00, or at any time on a weekend or public holiday, are
dispatched the next working day. Express orders placed after the cut-off cannot
be upgraded to same-day dispatch.

## Refrigerated goods

Dairy, seafood and fresh produce ship under the refrigerated service only. These
items cannot be combined with ambient goods in a single shipment; an order
containing both will be split into two shipments and two delivery charges apply.

Refrigerated shipments are not dispatched on Fridays, to avoid goods sitting in
a depot over the weekend. A refrigerated order placed on a Thursday afternoon
will therefore ship the following Monday.

## Delivery areas

Northwind delivers to all EU member states, the United Kingdom, Norway and
Switzerland. Deliveries to Switzerland and Norway attract customs handling of
25 euros per shipment and may be subject to import duty payable by the
recipient.

We do not deliver to the Canary Islands, Ceuta, Melilla, or to addresses outside
Europe. Requests for delivery outside our standard area should be referred to
the Commercial team, who may arrange a freight-forwarder shipment at cost.

## Damaged or missing shipments

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the
packaging and the goods. Claims made after 48 hours cannot be pursued with the
carrier and will normally be declined.

A shipment is treated as lost if it has not arrived within 10 working days of
dispatch for standard service, or 3 working days for express. Replacement
shipments for confirmed losses are sent at no charge under the express service.

## Order tracking

Tracking numbers are issued by email at dispatch. Tracking information may take
up to four hours after dispatch to appear on the carrier's system. Customers
contacting support before that window should be advised to wait rather than
having the shipment investigated.
