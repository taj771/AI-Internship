# Northwind Traders — Returns and Refunds

## Standard returns window

Ambient goods may be returned within 30 days of delivery provided the packaging
is unopened and undamaged. Returns received within 14 days of delivery are
refunded in full. Returns received between 15 and 30 days are subject to a
restocking fee of 15% of the order value.

Returns are not accepted after 30 days. This is a firm limit and Customer
Support cannot authorise an exception; requests beyond 30 days must be escalated
to the Commercial Director.

## Goods that cannot be returned

The following are non-returnable once dispatched, unless faulty:

- Refrigerated and frozen goods
- Fresh produce
- Products with fewer than 60 days remaining before their best-before date
- Items sold as clearance or end-of-line
- Bulk pallet orders that have been broken down

## Faulty or incorrect goods

Faulty goods, and goods sent in error, are returned at Northwind's cost and
refunded in full including original shipping. There is no restocking fee and the
30-day window does not apply, though claims should be raised promptly.

Photographic evidence is required before a return label is issued for faulty
goods. For refrigerated items reported as spoiled on arrival, support may issue
a refund without a physical return, since returning the goods serves no purpose.

## Refund timing

Approved refunds are processed within 5 working days of the returned goods
arriving at our warehouse, or of a no-return refund being authorised. The funds
typically reach the customer's account 3 to 5 working days after processing,
depending on their bank.

Refunds are always issued to the original payment method. Northwind does not
issue refunds by bank transfer to a different account, and does not issue store
credit unless the customer specifically requests it in place of a refund.

## Account customers

Customers on credit terms receive a credit note rather than a payment. Credit
notes are applied against the next invoice and expire 12 months after issue.

## Trade returns

Wholesale customers returning goods under a trade agreement should follow the
process in their contract, which may differ from this policy. Where the contract
is silent, this policy applies.
