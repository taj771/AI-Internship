# Northwind Traders — Customer Support Handbook

## Contact hours and channels

Customer Support operates 08:00 to 18:00 CET, Monday to Friday, excluding German
public holidays. There is no weekend cover. Out-of-hours messages are queued and
answered in order of arrival from 08:00 on the next working day.

Email and the web form are the primary channels. Telephone support is available
to account customers only, using the number printed on their invoice.

## Response targets

| Priority | Definition | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Refrigerated shipment at risk, or a food safety concern | 1 hour |
| P2 | Order not delivered, wrong goods received | 4 hours |
| P3 | Invoice query, general enquiry | 1 working day |
| P4 | Product information, quotes | 2 working days |

Response targets run only during contact hours. A P2 raised at 17:30 is due by
09:30 the following working day, not 21:30 the same evening.

## Escalation

Escalate to the Support Lead when a case has been open beyond twice its
response target, when a customer requests escalation explicitly, or when the
value in dispute exceeds 2,000 euros.

Food safety concerns are escalated immediately to the Quality Manager and do not
wait for the Support Lead. This is the only case where an agent should escalate
outside the support line.

## Goodwill gestures

Agents may issue a goodwill credit of up to 50 euros without approval, once per
customer per quarter. Credits between 50 and 250 euros require Support Lead
approval. Anything above 250 euros requires a director.

A goodwill credit is not an admission of liability and should never be described
as compensation in correspondence.

## Common questions

**Where is my order?** Check the tracking reference first. Carrier systems can
take up to four hours after dispatch to show movement, so a shipment dispatched
this morning may not yet be trackable.

**Can I change my delivery address after ordering?** Only before dispatch. Once
a shipment has left the warehouse the address is fixed and the order must be
refused on delivery and reordered.

**Do you ship outside Europe?** No. See the Shipping and Delivery Terms for the
full list of served countries.

**My refrigerated order arrived warm.** Treat as P1. Ask for photographs of the
packaging, the goods and any temperature indicator. Do not ask the customer to
return spoiled food.

**Can I get a VAT invoice?** Yes, available from the account portal
immediately after dispatch. Support can re-issue one on request.
