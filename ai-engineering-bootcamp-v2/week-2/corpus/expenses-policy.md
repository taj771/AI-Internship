# Northwind Traders — Expenses and Travel Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Supersedes the 2024 policy.

## Submitting a claim

All expense claims must be submitted through the Expenses portal with an
itemised receipt attached. Card statements are not accepted as receipts because
they do not show what was purchased.

Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.
Claims submitted after 30 days require written justification and approval from
the Finance Director, and may be declined.

Claims are reimbursed in the employee's payroll currency on the next payroll run
after approval, provided the claim is approved at least five working days before
the payroll cut-off.

## Approval thresholds

Expenses under 500 euros are approved by the employee's line manager. Any single
expense of 500 euros or more requires director sign-off, and that approval must
be obtained **before** the expense is incurred. Retrospective approval of an
expense over the threshold is at the Finance Director's discretion and is not
guaranteed.

Splitting a single purchase into multiple claims to stay below the threshold is
a disciplinary matter.

## Travel

Economy class is standard for all flights under six hours. Flights of six hours
or more may be booked in premium economy with director approval. Business class
is not reimbursed except where required as a documented accessibility
adjustment.

Rail travel should be booked at least seven days in advance where the journey is
planned. Standard class is the default; first class may be booked only where it
is cheaper than the standard fare at time of booking, which does occur on some
routes.

Hotel accommodation is capped at 180 euros per night in Berlin, London and
Paris, and 120 euros per night elsewhere in Europe. The cap is inclusive of
local taxes.

## Subsistence

The daily meal allowance while travelling is 55 euros, or 75 euros in the three
capped cities listed above. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances,
including when part of a client entertainment bill.

## Client entertainment

Client entertainment requires prior approval from a director regardless of
value. The claim must name every attendee and their organisation. Entertainment
of public officials is prohibited without prior sign-off from Legal, in line
with the Anti-Bribery Policy.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre. Mileage claims
must state the start point, destination and business purpose. Commuting between
home and an employee's normal place of work is never reimbursable.
