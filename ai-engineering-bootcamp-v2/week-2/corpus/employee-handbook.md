# Northwind Traders — Employee Handbook 2026

## Working arrangements

Northwind Traders operates a hybrid working model. Employees may work remotely
up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Tuesdays and Wednesdays are
designated collaboration days and all staff in commercial roles are expected
onsite on those days. Warehouse and logistics staff work onsite five days per
week because of the nature of the role.

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core
hours, staff may arrange their working day as they see fit provided weekly
contracted hours are met.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, plus public
holidays in their country of employment. Leave accrues monthly from the start
date at 2.08 days per month. Employees joining part-way through a year receive
a pro-rated entitlement.

Unused leave may be carried over into the following year up to a maximum of 5
days. Carried-over leave must be taken before 31 March or it is forfeited.
Northwind does not pay in lieu of untaken leave except on termination of
employment.

Leave requests of five consecutive days or more require four weeks' notice.
Requests during the November to December peak shipping period will normally be
declined for warehouse and logistics staff.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave at full pay.
Secondary caregivers are entitled to 8 weeks at full pay. Both entitlements
become available after 12 months of continuous service. Parental leave may be
taken in up to three separate blocks within the first two years following birth
or adoption.

## Sick leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A self-certified
absence may cover up to three consecutive working days. Absences beyond three
days require a medical certificate. Sick leave does not accrue and unused days
do not carry over.

## Probation and notice

New employees serve a six-month probation period during which the notice period
is two weeks on either side. After probation, the notice period is one month for
all staff below manager grade and three months for managers and above.

## Equipment

Northwind provides a laptop and one external monitor to all office-based staff.
Home office furniture, including chairs and desks, is not reimbursed. Employees
requiring specialist equipment for accessibility reasons should contact People
Operations, and such requests are handled outside the standard equipment policy.
