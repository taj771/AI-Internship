# Northwind Traders — Supplier Agreements and Procurement

## Supplier onboarding

New suppliers must complete a due diligence pack before the first purchase
order is raised. The pack covers company registration, VAT status, food safety
certification, insurance, and a signed copy of our Supplier Code of Conduct.

Food suppliers must hold a current BRCGS or IFS certificate at grade B or above.
A supplier whose certification lapses is suspended from new orders until it is
reinstated; existing orders in transit are unaffected.

Onboarding takes 4 to 6 weeks. Commercial teams should not commit to a supplier
or agree launch dates before onboarding is complete.

## Payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 45 days from invoice date. Terms of net 30 may be
agreed for suppliers who offer a corresponding discount of at least 1.5%. Terms
shorter than net 30 require Finance Director approval and are granted only where
the supplier is sole-source for a listed product.

Early settlement discounts are taken only where the discount exceeds our cost of
capital, currently assessed at 6% annually.

## Key suppliers

Exotic Liquids, based in London, supplies our beverage and syrup lines including
Chai, Chang and Aniseed Syrup. The agreement runs to 31 December 2027 and
includes exclusivity for Chang in the German and Austrian markets.

Formaggi Fortini, based in Ravenna, supplies Italian cheeses under refrigerated
terms. Minimum order quantity is 40 kilograms per line. Lead time is 12 working
days and cannot be expedited during August, when the producer closes.

Tokyo Traders supplies frozen seafood on 60-day terms, negotiated as an
exception in 2024 because of the shipping lead time from Japan. This is the only
supplier on terms longer than net 45.

## Price changes

Suppliers must give 90 days' written notice of any price increase. Increases
notified with less than 90 days' notice may be rejected, and the previous price
holds for the remainder of the notice period.

Northwind reviews the full supplier price list twice yearly, in March and
September. Ad-hoc increases outside those windows are accepted only where driven
by a documented commodity movement of more than 10%.

## Quality incidents

A quality incident is any batch that fails inspection on arrival, or any customer
complaint traced to a supplier's product. Two incidents within a rolling 12
months trigger a formal supplier review. Three trigger suspension pending a
corrective action plan.

Costs of a recall caused by a supplier defect are recoverable under clause 9 of
the standard supply agreement, including our handling costs and customer
compensation.
